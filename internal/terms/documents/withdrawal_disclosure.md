# Withdrawal and Verification Disclosure

**Status: draft, pending review by qualified counsel. This document has not been
reviewed by a lawyer and is not a final disclosure.**

This document is shown before a withdrawal or conversion request, not at signup,
because nothing about it applies until you ask to take value out.

## Nodal does not move money

Nodal is not a money transmitter. Where value leaves the internal economy, a
licensed provider performs the transfer and Nodal records what that provider
did. Nodal has no ability to convert Credits into money by itself, and the
system deliberately contains no primitive that would let it.

## You will be asked to verify your identity

A conversion or payout requires identity verification performed by a provider on
its own pages. Nodal receives the provider's decision. Nodal does not receive,
store, or ask you to upload identity documents.

## A request is not a payment

A submitted request is a request. It can be refused for reasons including
jurisdiction, verification outcome, risk review, the origin of the units
involved, or a control being switched off. Where a request is refused, the
product tells you the reason it is permitted to give.

## Timing

A request that is accepted still passes through reserve, provider settlement and
reconciliation before value arrives. None of those steps is instant, and none of
them is guaranteed to complete.

## Fees

Where a provider charges a fee, the amount you receive is quoted net of it
before you confirm. Nodal never shows a gross figure as though it were what you
will receive.

## What this draft does not yet say

The specific provider, the jurisdictions served, the fee schedule, and the
complaint route. Each depends on a commercial arrangement that does not exist
yet, and inventing them here would be a fabrication.
