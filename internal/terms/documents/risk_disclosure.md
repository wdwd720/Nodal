# Risk Disclosure

**Status: draft, pending review by qualified counsel. This document has not been
reviewed by a lawyer and is not a final risk disclosure.**

Read this before using any market on Nodal.

## You can lose what you put in

Internal markets on Nodal are speculative. Prices are set by the participants,
not by an outside reference, and there is no floor under them. A position can go
to zero. Nodal makes no representation that any asset has value, that any market
is liquid, or that any price is fair.

## These are not investments

Nothing on Nodal is an investment product, and nothing here is investment
advice, a recommendation, or a solicitation. Nodal does not manage anything on
your behalf and does not act as your adviser.

## Internal markets are thin

An internal market may have very little liquidity. A small order can move the
price a long way; a large one may not fill at all. Price impact shown before a
trade is an estimate from the state of the market at that instant, not a promise.

## Automated strategies do not make it safer

Where the product lets an automated strategy propose an action, the strategy
proposes and deterministic controls decide. An automated strategy has no
privileged information, cannot sign anything, cannot withdraw anything, and does
not reduce any of the risks on this page.

## Simulation is not live

Results produced in simulation or on a sandbox tier were produced by a system
configured not to move value. They say nothing about what would have happened
live and must never be presented as though they do.

## Availability

A market can be halted, an asset restricted, and a capability switched off,
without notice, when a control requires it. You may be unable to trade or to
exit a position at a moment when you want to.
