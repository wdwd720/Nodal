# Credits Terms

**Status: draft, pending review by qualified counsel. This document has not been
reviewed by a lawyer and is not a final agreement.**

This is the document the product asks you to read before you hold Credits, and
the one sentence that matters is the first one.

## Credits are internal and are not withdrawable

A Credit is a unit of account inside Nodal. It is not money, not a deposit, not
stored value you can demand back, not a security, and not a token on any chain.
Buying Credits is not funding an account you can empty again on request.

## Where a Credit came from is part of what it is

The ledger records the origin of every unit — purchased, promotional, a refund,
an earning from something you sold, proceeds from an internal market, an
administrative adjustment. Origin is never inferred; it is recorded by the event
that produced the unit, and it is the basis of every rule below.

## Conversion to money, if it ever happens, is separately controlled

Whether any unit may ever leave the internal economy depends on a named,
versioned policy, on identity verification, on the jurisdiction you are in, and
on a capability that is switched on only through a control ceremony requiring
multiple people and external approvals. Today, for most origins and in most
configurations, the answer is no. The product shows you which of your balance,
if any, is eligible right now; that number and your total balance are different
numbers and are always displayed as such.

## Promotional and adjusted Credits

Credits granted promotionally, refunded, or adjusted by an operator are not
convertible to money by any policy in this system. They exist so you can use the
product.

## No interest, no yield

Credits earn nothing by being held. Nodal pays no interest and offers no yield.

## If Nodal stops operating

Credits are an internal record. This draft makes no promise about what happens
to them if the service ends; what such a promise should say is a question for
counsel and is deliberately left open rather than answered here.
